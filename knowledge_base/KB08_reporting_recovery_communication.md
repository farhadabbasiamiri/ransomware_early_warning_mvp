# Reporting, recovery and communication

## Reporting
In Canada, the Cyber Centre playbook advises reporting ransomware to local police, the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre and the Cyber Centre (online reporting portal). In the United States, CISA and the FBI accept reports. Follow internal escalation, legal and privacy notification requirements, especially if data may have been stolen.

## Recovery
Rebuild affected systems from known-good images, restore data from verified clean backups, and reset credentials before reconnecting systems. Restore critical services first according to the business continuity plan. Monitor restored systems closely for re-infection.

## Communication
Use out-of-band communication channels if the corporate network or email may be compromised. Keep stakeholders informed with factual, approved updates. After recovery, hold a lessons-learned review and update detection rules and this playbook.
